💪 Push / Pull / Legs

Overview: PPL is the most popular hypertrophy split. Each session targets one movement pattern, allowing maximum volume per muscle group with adequate recovery.

Weekly Structure

DayFocusKey Exercises
Day 1PushBench Press, OHP, Incline DB Press, Lateral Raise, Tricep Pushdown
Day 2PullDeadlift, Pull-ups, Cable Row, Face Pull, Bicep Curl
Day 3LegsSquat, Leg Press, Romanian DL, Leg Curl, Calf Raise
Day 4–7RestActive recovery, stretching, walks

Target Parameters

  • Sets: 4–5 per exercise
  • Reps: 8–12 (hypertrophy range)
  • Rest: 60–90 seconds
  • Progressive overload: Add 2.5 kg when you hit top of rep range 2 sessions in a row

🏋️ Upper / Lower Split

Overview: Trains each muscle group twice per week. Ideal for building both strength and hypertrophy with good frequency.

🏆 3-Day Full Body

Overview: The best beginner program. Hit every muscle 3× per week with compound movements. Simple, effective, proven.

Weekly Structure

DayFocusKey Exercises
MonFull Body ASquat, Bench Press, Bent-over Row
WedFull Body BDeadlift, OHP, Pull-up / Lat Pulldown
FriFull Body ARepeat A (add weight)

Target Parameters

  • Sets: 3 per exercise
  • Reps: 5–8 (strength) or 8–12 (hypertrophy)
  • Rest: 2–3 minutes between sets
  • Tip: Alternate A/B each session for variety

🔥 HIIT Fat Burn

Overview: High-intensity intervals mixed with compound strength work. Burns maximum calories in minimal time with EPOC (afterburn) effect.

Weekly Structure

DayFocusKey Exercises
MonHIITBurpees, Jump Squat, Mountain Climber (40s on / 20s off × 6 rounds)
TueStrengthDB Press, Rows, Pushups, Core work
WedActive RestYoga / Walking / Stretching
ThuHIITPlank, Bicycle Crunch, KB Swings (core focus)
FriStrengthGoblet Squat, Lunge, Hip Thrust, Leg Curl

Target Parameters

  • Work interval: 40 seconds on, 20 seconds off
  • Rounds: 5–8 per circuit
  • Rest: 1 min between circuits
  • Tip: Combine with 200–300 kcal daily deficit for best fat loss

⚡ Strength 5×5

Overview: Based on StrongLifts 5×5 — the simplest and most effective program to build raw strength. Focus on compound barbell movements and linear progression.

Weekly Structure

DayFocusKey Exercises
MonWorkout ASquat 5×5, Bench Press 5×5, Barbell Row 5×5
WedWorkout BSquat 5×5, OHP 5×5, Deadlift 1×5
FriWorkout ARepeat A (add +2.5 kg to each lift)

Target Parameters

  • Sets: 5 × 5 reps (1 × 5 for Deadlift)
  • Rest: 3–5 minutes between sets
  • Progression: Add 2.5 kg every session — the key to this program
  • When to deload: If you fail to complete 5 reps 3 sessions in a row, drop weight by 10%
